CircuitLore — Initial Functional Requirements

Course: CSE 499 — Senior Project
Project Name: CircuitLore
Developer: Elora Mathias

Project Overview

CircuitLore is an interactive educational website designed to teach students ages 10–14 about electricity. The application will combine the history of electricity with modern electrical concepts, interactive lessons, quizzes, and a virtual circuit-building lab.

One of the main goals of CircuitLore is to make learning about electricity more interesting and hands-on. Students will meet historical figures, learn about their discoveries, and see how those discoveries connect to the electricity we use today. Students will also be able to experiment with virtual electrical circuits in a safe environment.

The application will save student progress so users can return and continue learning where they left off. Parents or teachers will also have the ability to view the progress of students connected to their accounts.

Functional Requirements

FR-01: User Accounts and Authentication

The system shall allow students to create an account.

The system shall allow registered users to log in and log out.

The system shall allow users to update their basic profile information.

The system shall allow users to delete their accounts.

The system shall provide different access permissions for students, adults, and administrators.

FR-02: Learning Journey

The system shall provide an organized learning journey that students can follow.

The system shall display lessons in a defined order, beginning with historical discoveries and progressing toward modern electricity.

The system shall allow students to view previously completed lessons.

The system shall unlock new learning content as students complete required activities.

The system shall display the student's current position in the learning journey.

FR-03: Historical Learning Experience

The system shall introduce important historical figures who contributed to the understanding of electricity.

The system shall display historical information through interactive scenes, characters, and dialogue.

The system shall allow students to move through historical stories at their own pace.

The system shall explain how historical discoveries contributed to modern electrical technology.

The system shall include visual demonstrations or animations to support historical lessons.

FR-04: Electricity Lessons

The system shall provide educational lessons covering basic electricity concepts.

The system shall explain concepts such as electrical current, voltage, resistance, conductors, insulators, and circuits.

The system shall display written explanations and visual examples.

The system shall allow students to complete lessons and continue to the next learning activity.

The system shall connect electrical concepts to real-world examples.

FR-05: Interactive Activities and Quizzes

The system shall provide interactive activities and quizzes related to lesson content.

The system shall support multiple-choice and true/false questions.

The system shall check student answers and provide immediate feedback.

The system shall allow students to retry appropriate activities or quizzes.

The system shall calculate quiz scores and save results to the student's account.

FR-06: Student Progress Tracking

The system shall create progress records for students as they begin learning activities.

The system shall save completed lessons, quizzes, and circuit challenges.

The system shall retrieve saved progress when a student logs back into the application.

The system shall update progress as students complete additional learning activities.

The system shall display overall learning progress using a percentage or progress bar.

The system shall allow students to reset or delete their learning progress after confirmation.

FR-07: Virtual Circuit Lab

The system shall provide an interactive workspace where students can build basic electrical circuits.

The system shall allow students to add components such as batteries, wires, light bulbs, switches, and resistors.

The system shall allow students to move, connect, and remove electrical components.

The system shall allow students to open and close switches.

The system shall allow students to test their circuits.

The system shall allow students to clear or reset the circuit workspace.

FR-08: Circuit Validation and Feedback

The system shall analyze the electrical components and connections within a student's circuit.

The system shall determine whether a basic circuit has a complete electrical path.

The system shall recognize open and closed switches.

The system shall visually turn a light bulb on when the required electrical circuit is complete.

The system shall provide feedback when a circuit is incomplete or incorrectly connected.

The system shall determine whether a student has successfully completed a required circuit challenge.

FR-09: Parent and Teacher Dashboard

The system shall allow authorized adults to connect their accounts to student accounts.

The system shall allow adults to view the learning progress of connected students.

The system shall display completed lessons, quiz results, and circuit challenges.

The system shall prevent adults from viewing students who are not connected to their accounts.

The system shall allow an adult-student connection to be removed without deleting the student's learning progress.

FR-10: AI Learning Assistant

The system shall allow students to request additional help while completing lessons or activities.

The system shall provide age-appropriate explanations and hints related to electricity.

The system shall use the current lesson or circuit validation results to provide relevant assistance.

The system shall encourage students to solve problems rather than immediately providing answers.

The system shall allow students to continue using the core learning features when AI assistance is unavailable.

FR-11: Animation and Visual Experience

The system shall display historical characters and environments using a consistent realistic-cartoon visual style.

The system shall use animations to introduce characters and demonstrate important electrical concepts.

The system shall provide visual transitions between historical discoveries and modern electricity lessons.

The system shall provide visual feedback when students complete lessons, activities, or challenges.

The system shall allow important learning content to remain accessible without requiring animations.

FR-12: Learning Content Management

The system shall allow authorized administrators to create educational lessons and learning content.

The system shall allow administrators to view and edit existing learning content.

The system shall allow administrators to delete or archive appropriate learning content.

The system shall allow administrators to manage quiz questions and circuit challenges.

The system shall allow administrators to control the order in which learning content appears.

The system shall prevent students and unauthorized users from modifying official learning content.

Conclusion

These initial functional requirements outline the main features I plan to develop for CircuitLore. My primary focus will be creating a working educational experience that combines historical storytelling, electricity lessons, interactive activities, circuit building, and saved student progress.

As development continues, I may need to adjust the scope of certain features based on the time available and what I learn during implementation. The full requirements document in Week 07 will expand on these requirements and include additional details about the application's behavior, constraints, and testing expectations.
